How to use the color contrast checker


  1. Enter your text color and background color, or pick them with the color pickers.
  2. Read the contrast ratio and the pass or fail result for AA and AAA, normal and large text, and UI elements.
  3. Check the live preview to see headings, body text, small print and a button in your colors.
  4. If it fails, click Fix contrast for me to get the nearest passing text color that keeps your hue.


Frequently asked questions


What contrast ratio do I need for WCAG AA?
WCAG AA requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (about 24px, or 19px bold) and for icons and UI components. AAA requires 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.
How is the contrast ratio calculated?
It compares the relative luminance of the two colors using the formula in the WCAG specification, giving a ratio from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white).
Does the fix button change my brand color?
It only adjusts lightness of the text color and keeps the hue and saturation, so the result still looks like your color, just readable.
Is contrast a legal requirement?
In many places accessibility laws reference WCAG, including the ADA in the US and the European Accessibility Act. Meeting AA contrast is a core part of compliance.

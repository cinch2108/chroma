How to use the image color picker


  1. Drag an image onto the page, paste one with Ctrl+V or ⌘V, or click to upload.
  2. Chroma extracts the dominant colors and shows how much of the image each one covers.
  3. Use the slider to get between 3 and 10 colors.
  4. Hover the image to see a magnified color preview and click to copy any exact pixel.
  5. Send the extracted colors to the palette editor to refine and export them.


Frequently asked questions


Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No. The image is read and analysed entirely inside your browser and never sent to a server.
How are the palette colors chosen?
Chroma groups similar pixels with k-means clustering in the OKLab color space, which matches human perception, and ranks colors by how much of the image they cover.
Which image formats work?
PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP images up to 25 MB.
Can I get the hex code of one exact pixel?
Yes. Hover over the image to preview, then click to copy that pixel’s hex code.

Privacy

Chroma runs entirely in your browser. Colors you create and images you open are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us.

What's stored

Saved palettes and your cookie choice are kept in your browser's local storage. You can clear them anytime by clearing site data.

Advertising

Chroma is free because it shows ads. Ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. If you choose "Essential only", only non-personalised ads are shown. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.
